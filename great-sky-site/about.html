From NIST to Great Sky: A Decade in the Making

Boulder, Colorado · est. 2024

Great Sky began at the National Institute of Standards and Technology, America's national laboratory in Boulder, Colorado.

For over a decade, the team developed, tested, and refined the core technology, supported by NIST's world-class facilities and research mandate.

Great Sky was founded in late 2024 with the mission to harnesses the power of physics to build era-defining computational intelligence.

Join Us →

Great Sky envisions a future where AI is unencumbered by legacy computing: powerful enough to appreciate every individual, continuously adaptive to integrate collective wisdom, and so efficient it spans the globe without straining resources.
