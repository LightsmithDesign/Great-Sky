Privacy Policy

How Great Sky AI collects, uses, and protects information about visitors to this site and people who contact us.

Effective date: 21 May 2026

Great Sky AI ("Great Sky," "we," "us") builds AI hardware and operates this website. This policy explains what information we collect from visitors to greatskyai.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the name, email address, and any details you include in your message. When you sign up for updates, we collect your email address. We collect this information only when you choose to provide it.

We also use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights) to understand aggregate site traffic and performance — which pages are visited and how quickly they load. This data is aggregated and does not identify you individually. Our web host also processes standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and reliability.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to respond to your inquiries, send company updates if you have signed up for them, operate and improve this website, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and our analytics are cookieless. We use only a small number of strictly necessary cookies, where required for site functionality and for staff who log into our content management system.

How we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the site, and only as needed: Vercel (website hosting and analytics), Supabase (database storage for form submissions and subscriptions), and Resend (delivery of transactional and update emails). We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

Some pages link to third-party sites — for example, job openings link to an external application system — and information you submit there is governed by their privacy policies, not this one.

Data retention

We keep contact-form submissions and subscriber information for as long as needed to respond to you, send updates you have requested, and meet our legal and operational needs.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe from update emails at any time using the link in any email, and you can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the EU/UK GDPR; contact us to exercise them and we will respond as required by applicable law.

Data security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls, to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and limit access to it.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above and post the updated version on this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or the information we hold about you? Please reach us through the contact form on this site.
