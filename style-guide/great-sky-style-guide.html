Foundation

Design principles

The system is built from first principles — every choice earns its place. Restraint, editorial rhythm, and physical materiality guide the whole.

01
Editorial, not decorative
Generous whitespace, a serif display voice, and hairline rules. The page reads like a printed journal, not a dashboard.
02
Warm neutrals, one deep accent
A cream-to-stone field grounded by olive and near-black ink. Color is structural, never ornamental.
03
Materiality & restraint
Film grain, subtle grayscale imagery, and slow physical easing lend quiet texture without noise.

The 60-30-10 balance

60% — cream neutral fields carry the majority of every layout.

30% — ink & olive for type, dark sections, and primary actions.

10% — sage & stone for labels, accents, and italic emphasis.

Soft text and hairlines are never flat greys — they are mixes of ink into the warm field, keeping the whole palette in one temperature.

Typography · Conventions

Italic accent & the eyebrow

The italic emphasis

A decade in the making

Headlines pair upright roman with an italic phrase in olive (or sage on dark). It carries the emotional accent of every display line.

The mono eyebrow

Announcement · 8 min read

Small uppercase monospace labels with wide 0.15–0.22em tracking mark sections, metadata, and categories. Always sage on dark fields.

Tracking scales with size: tighter for large display (−0.015em), wide and airy for small labels (up to 0.22em).

Imagery

Photographic treatment

Imagery is muted to sit inside the palette — desaturated, slightly contrast-lifted, and cropped to consistent frames.

A near-invisible film grain (≈3% opacity) drifts over dark surfaces, giving the whole site an analog, printed texture.

Motion

Easing & reveal

Motion is slow, weighted, and physical. Two custom curves and a single reveal pattern govern the entire experience.

Primary ease
cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)
The signature — decisive start, long luxurious settle. Buttons, headers, underline sweeps.
Soft ease
cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.46, 0.2, 1)
Gentler support curve for color, opacity, and background transitions.

Content fades up on scroll via a staggered reveal — 0.8s, small translate, sequenced by data-delay. Durations run long (0.6–0.9s); nothing snaps.
